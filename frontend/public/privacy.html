Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Last Updated: October 23, 2026

LaTrobe Irrigation ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LaTrobe Irrigation Project Management and Field Operations Assistant application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our internal operational platform and its integrations with third-party services, including Intuit QuickBooks Online.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of information to provide operational and project management workflows:

  • QuickBooks Online Data: When connected via Intuit OAuth 2.0 authorization, our Service syncs accounting and project entities, including Customer names, billing/shipping contact details, project job identifiers, and item/materials catalog data (descriptions, unit rates, and inventory classifications).
  • Operational & Field Data: Field shift logs, technician labor hours, material usage logs, project status updates, and on-site notes submitted by authorized team members.
  • Communication & Bot Data: Telegram user IDs, usernames, and operational commands submitted through our authorized operations bot.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively for internal business and operational purposes:

  • Synchronizing project details and materials catalogs between QuickBooks Online and field personnel.
  • Accurately tracking project progress, job costs, and inventory allocation.
  • Assisting authorized team members with project scheduling, calendar bookings, and job queries.
  • Maintaining data integrity and consistency across internal operational databases.

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your data. We do not disclose any QuickBooks customer or financial data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

3. Data Storage and Security

We treat data security with strict industry standards:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: All synced project and catalog data is securely stored within Google Cloud Platform / Firebase Firestore, utilizing AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  • OAuth Security: Integration with QuickBooks Online is governed by Intuit OAuth 2.0 standards. Access tokens are short-lived, and refresh tokens are stored securely in encrypted backend storage.
  • Access Controls: System access is strictly restricted to authorized LaTrobe Irrigation staff via authenticated access control lists.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We only share data with essential infrastructure service providers necessary to deliver the Service:

  • Intuit Inc. (QuickBooks Online): For accounting data synchronization via official Intuit REST APIs.
  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: For encrypted database hosting and infrastructure.
  • Google Workspace (Calendar): For dispatch and project schedule coordination.

5. Data Retention, Disconnection, and Deletion

You or your authorized administrator can disconnect the QuickBooks Online integration at any time through the Intuit App Management portal or within our internal settings.

  • Disconnection: Disconnecting the integration immediately revokes and invalidates all OAuth tokens. No further data sync calls will be executed.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request the deletion of any cached or stored synchronized data by contacting us at the email provided below. Requests will be executed within 30 days, subject to legal or statutory tax retention obligations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The latest revision date will always be indicated at the top of this document.
